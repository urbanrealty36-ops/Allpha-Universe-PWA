# V2.13D.3 — Human Visual Fidelity Review & Storage Promotion Gate

## Objective
Convert the GREEN V2.13D.2 preflight into an auditable human visual-fidelity and promotion decision without rebuilding the 25×14 production pack and without mutating Supabase during review.

## Locked evidence
- V2.13D.2 isolated preflight: GREEN.
- V2.13D.1A-R3 automated Golden Gate: GREEN.
- 25 themes / 14 categories / 350 assets / 350 previews / 25 contact sheets.
- Golden benchmark: Crystal AI City.
- Canonical renderer: AllphaWorldRenderer.

## Human review criteria
1. Theme identity and structural differentiation.
2. Geometry quality and spatial hierarchy.
3. Material/PBR readability and non-flat presentation.
4. Lighting, atmosphere, camera composition, and cinematic depth.
5. Category fidelity across all 14 categories.
6. No obvious broken geometry, missing asset, unreadable preview, or placeholder treatment.
7. Mobile/browser presentation plausibility and performance awareness.

## Approval contract
Two explicit approvals are required:
- `ALLPHA_3D_HUMAN_VISUAL_FIDELITY_APPROVED=true`
- `ALLPHA_3D_PRODUCTION_PROMOTION_APPROVED=true`

The workflow remains successful while approvals are pending, but the phase remains BLOCKED. Only when both approvals are present does the review report become `READY_FOR_CONTROLLED_PROMOTION`.

## Safety boundary
- This phase performs **no Supabase Storage mutation**.
- No service-role secret is exposed to the frontend.
- The next storage mutation must be performed only by a controlled backend/activation job after this gate is explicitly approved.
- GREEN evidence from previous phases is reused and is not rebuilt.
