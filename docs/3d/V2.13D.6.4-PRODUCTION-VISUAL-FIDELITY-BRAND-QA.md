## What changed

### 1. Production camera auto-framing
The real loaded GLB is measured after its presentation-only normalization. The runtime computes:

- world-space bounding box;
- bounding sphere;
- responsive portrait/landscape margin;
- target elevation;
- camera distance from the actual asset radius and FOV.

The production PerspectiveCamera is then positioned and aimed at the real asset composition. This removes dependence on a single hard-coded camera distance while preserving the canonical renderer.

### 2. Production material presentation pass
The real GLB materials are cloned before presentation normalization. The pass:

- preserves authored material type/textures;
- clamps roughness to a production-readable range;
- bounds metalness;
- applies bounded environment-map intensity;
- bounds emissive intensity;
- disables depth writing for authored transparent materials;
- marks normalized materials for update.

No new geometry or fake material assets are generated.

### 3. Allpha Universe visual evidence marker
The runtime marker now exposes:

- real mesh/object/material counts;
- geometry bounds;
- camera distance/FOV/aspect/target;
- `ALLPHA_UNIVERSE_V2` visual profile;
- ACES Filmic tone mapping contract;
- sRGB output color-space contract;
- production exposure.

These values are evidence hooks only; they are not business state.

### 4. Automated D6.4 browser gate
Added:
- `tests/d6-4-production-visual-fidelity.spec.ts`
- `.github/workflows/v2-13d6-4-production-visual-fidelity.yml`

The gate checks desktop and mobile production World rendering, real V2.13 GLB download, camera/material evidence, color-management markers, WebGL visibility, luminance ratio/variance, and browser errors. It captures desktop/mobile screenshots as artifacts.
