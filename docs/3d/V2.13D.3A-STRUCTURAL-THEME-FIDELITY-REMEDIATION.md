# V2.13D.3A — Structural Theme Fidelity Remediation

## Objective
Remediate the human-review failure from V2.13D.3 by changing world-scale spatial identity, not merely palette/material styling.

## Locked evidence
V2.13D.1A-R3 and V2.13D.2 remain locked. This phase does not rebuild the 350-asset production pack.

## Scope
Only the four failed world-scale categories are regenerated:
- Universe
- Galaxy
- World
- District

Scope: 25 themes × 4 categories = 100 remediated assets.
The remaining 250 V2.13D assets are reused as locked evidence.

## Structural strategy
- Universe: asymmetrical macro landmark, orbital bands, suspended districts, bridges.
- Galaxy: constellation/node graph and navigational links instead of concentric rail composition.
- World: terrain/habitat silhouette, landmark, directional axes and portal.
- District: street/courtyard/block/harbor/vertical grammar based on theme district semantics.
- Theme families receive structural silhouette cues for Japanese, desert/ancient, organic, oceanic, industrial/heroic, neon/megacity, quantum/temporal and other families.
