## Strongest evidence

Across the 25 contact sheets, the **universe, galaxy, world and district** previews repeatedly retain essentially the same large cylindrical/rail spatial grammar, with differences primarily introduced through palette, lighting and a limited set of attached forms.

Representative structural similarity was also observed programmatically during review:

- universe cross-theme average perceptual hash distance: **0.061**
- galaxy: **0.061**
- world: **0.071**
- district: **0.071**

By contrast, theme-specific categories show substantially larger visual variation:

- agent-character: **0.398**
- booth: **0.298**
- live-stage: **0.261**

This supports the human visual finding: **the theme factory currently differentiates object-level accents more strongly than world-scale spatial identity.**

## Crystal AI City benchmark finding

Crystal AI City is currently a useful **visual benchmark reference**, but its own generated previews are also visibly procedural and primitive relative to the final production-realistic art direction.

Therefore this review does **not** promote the current Crystal asset itself as production-complete. It remains the canonical benchmark reference while the next remediation wave raises the structural and cinematic quality of the entire family.

## Required remediation before promotion

1. Introduce genuinely theme-specific **world-scale geometry grammar** for Universe, Galaxy, World and District.
2. Ensure landmark, architecture, silhouette and spatial hierarchy differ by theme—not only materials/colors.
3. Preserve the 14-category contract and 25-theme matrix.
4. Keep Crystal AI City as the Golden Benchmark while improving it where necessary.
5. Regenerate **only the affected assets/categories**; do not blindly rebuild already-valid evidence.
6. Re-run only the relevant validation/review gates after remediation.
7. Keep Supabase Storage promotion blocked until the human fidelity decision becomes APPROVED.
