## Findings

### 1. Theme identity / structural differentiation — FAIL

The 25 themes still read as variations of one dominant procedural spatial grammar. The large tubular/arched/cylindrical composition remains visually dominant across Universe, Galaxy, World and District.

Theme family changes are present, but they are not strong enough at first glance to establish 25 distinct world-scale identities.

### 2. Geometry / spatial hierarchy — FAIL

The intended D3A structural heroes are not consistently visually dominant in the final camera framing. The common base massing occupies most of the image, reducing the perceptual impact of the theme-specific structures.

### 3. Silhouette / massing — FAIL

Cross-theme silhouettes remain highly similar. Differences are often visible only after close inspection rather than at the contact-sheet / first-impression level required for a world-scale theme system.

### 4. Negative space / composition — FAIL

Camera framing and common background geometry preserve a similar visual rhythm across themes. Theme-specific negative-space signatures are not sufficiently legible.

### 5. Material / PBR — PARTIAL

Materials, reflections and theme accents are coherent, but material variation does not compensate for insufficient structural differentiation.

### 6. Lighting / atmosphere / camera — PARTIAL

Lighting and camera variations are present, but the shared composition still dominates the perceived result.

### 7. Category fidelity — FAIL

Universe, Galaxy, World and District are not sufficiently distinguishable as different spatial scales and semantic experiences from the rendered previews alone.

### 8. Completeness — PASS

All 25 themes and all 4 remediated macro categories are represented: **100/100**.

### 9. Mobile/browser plausibility — PARTIAL

The previews remain plausible as browser-renderable assets, but the current composition does not establish the desired premium Allpha Universe visual language.

## Decision

**HUMAN VISUAL FIDELITY REVIEW: REJECTED**

D3A successfully passed its automated structural gate, but the human visual review does not meet the production-realistic Golden Benchmark bar.

This is a fidelity rejection, not an asset-count or pipeline-completeness failure.
